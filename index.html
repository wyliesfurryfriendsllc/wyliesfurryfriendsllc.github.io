About Me

Hi, I'm Xiaotong —
and I love your pets.

I have a deep fondness for animals and share my home with two cats. I also had a dog growing up, and have cared for hamsters from newborns. Every pet I've looked after has been happy to snuggle up to me — and that's something I'm truly proud of.

When I care for your pet, I don't just do the basics. I proactively clean food and water bowls, wipe paws after every walk, send photos and videos throughout each visit, and pay close attention to each pet's unique personality and habits.
